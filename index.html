The F3 way

Five core principles

Every F3 workout in the country runs on the same five rules. That's what makes it work.

Free of charge

No dues, no fees, no gym membership. Ever.

Open to all men

Every age, every fitness level. You'll be welcomed and pushed.

Held outdoors

Rain or shine, heat or cold. The weather is part of it.

Peer led

No trainers. The men take turns leading (the "Q").

Circle of Trust

Every workout ends in a circle: names, announcements, a word of encouragement.

Your first workout

Three steps. That's it.

You don't need to be in shape to start. You don't need to know anyone. Every man there was new once.

  • Pick a workout from the schedule. Most start at 5:30 AM and last 45 minutes.
  • Show up 5 minutes early. Tell the group you're new. Someone will walk you through it.
  • Stay for the Circle of Trust at the end. You'll leave with an F3 nickname.
